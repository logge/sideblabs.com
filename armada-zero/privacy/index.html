Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Armada Zero: Firestorm is still in development and hasn't been released. This policy describes the game as it's planned. If the plans change, the policy will be updated here before the game comes out.

In short: the game collects nothing about you. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account with me. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games for highscores and achievements, Google handles those.

Armada Zero: Firestorm is a game for Android and Windows, made by Peter Logarn. This policy covers the game and this website.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or any other data about you. It has:

  • no advertising;
  • no analytics or tracking;
  • no account or sign-in with me;
  • no server of mine. There isn't one.

What stays on your device

Your progress and your settings are saved on your own device. They are never sent to me. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Playing together

In co-op, the devices find each other on your own wifi network and talk directly to each other. What they send is the game itself: where the ships are, what they fire, and what each player has picked. None of it goes to me or to any server on the internet.

On Android the game has permission to use the network for this, and for Google Play Games if you sign in. It uses the network for nothing else.

Google Play Games

On Android I plan to use Google Play Games for highscores and achievements. It will be optional: the game plays the same without it.

If you sign in, Google keeps your scores and achievements with your Play Games profile, under Google's privacy policy. A highscore you post is shown to other players with your Play Games name, the way you've set your profile up with Google. The game gets your Play Games name and player ID from Google so that it can show your name and post your scores. They stay on your device and are not sent to me.

Other companies

The game has no code for advertising, analytics or tracking.

Google Play, where the Android version will be published, has a privacy policy of its own: policies.google.com/privacy. Google Play shows developers summary figures such as how many times a game has been installed, and crash reports from people who have agreed to share diagnostics with Google. They don't tell me who you are.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and its fonts and pictures are its own. It loads nothing from any other site.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Armada Zero: Firestorm isn't made for children in particular. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

The game is in development, so this policy may change before it's released. Any change is made on this page, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the game: use the contact form at Side B Labs.
