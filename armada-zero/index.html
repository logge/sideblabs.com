The game

How it plays

Armada Zero: Firestorm is a co-op space shooter in pixel art. An old war fleet is waking up, system by system, and you fly the last strike wing that can stop it.

1

One to four players

One device each, on the same wifi. Phones and computers can play together, and it works fine alone too.

2

Six weapons at once

Your ship carries up to six weapons at the same time, all powered by one reactor.

3

A lot of bullets

The enemy fire gets thicker the further you get. You have shields, bombs and a dash to get through it.

4

Better together

Hit an enemy from two sides and it takes more damage. When someone goes down, a friend can fly over and bring them back.

5

Short runs

A run is a strike into the fleet that ends at a capital ship. Between the fights you pick an upgrade card, and what you earn stays with you for the next run.

6

The pilots talk

The story is told over the radio while you fly, and every line is voiced.
