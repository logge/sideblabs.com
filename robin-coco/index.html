Coco, you're a great listener.

Shipwrecked, in pixel art

Robin was shipwrecked on a tiny island. Her only company is Coco, a coconut with a face, and a very good listener.

Every day she has a plan, to get off the island or at least to make the most of it. Every evening it goes hilariously wrong.

She lives on your watch as a watch face, and on your phone as a live wallpaper. Glance at either, any time, and you'll see where she's got to.

Poke her and she'll tell you what she thinks of that.

A new plan every day

And how it goes wrong

  • A raft to sail home on.

    It sinks under her.

  • A bonfire big enough for ships to see.

    One does, and puts it out with its fire hose.

  • HI in pebbles for passing planes.

    A flying saucer reads it, and beams up Coco.

  • A time machine made from a barrel.

    It works: she climbs in at dusk and comes out tomorrow.

  • An island election.

    The seagull votes for the crab.

  • A ship for Christmas.

    Santa comes, with the ship she wished for. In a bottle.

Thirty-one plans, a different one each day, with her birthday, Midsummer, Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve on their own days. Some nights she even gets away, and Coco has the beach to himself until she's back in the morning, none the better for it.

On your watch

A watch face for Wear OS

Robin on her island with the same story as on your phone, no phone needed. In between, around thirty everyday things, now and then with a word about it in a speech bubble.

Always on, the screen keeps the time, the moon in its phase, and Robin.

She keeps an eye on what your watch knows

  • On the hour, she checks the time.
  • Heart rate up? She works out with you, laps of the island.
  • Reach your step goal and she dances. Her hut goes up as your steps add up.
  • Unread notifications? A bottle washes up.
  • Rain on the way, snow, cold or heat, she notices.
  • In the morning, coffee. After your bedtime, she sleeps.

On your phone

The app, and your live wallpaper

On your phone, the island is the app and your live wallpaper, under the real sky: day, dusk and night, the moon in its phase, and your weather if you like. Rain, snow, clouds, sun.

Robin walks, swims and changes in her hut between one thing and the next. She sleeps until your alarm, and dances to your music.

What she's working on stands in the sand all day, and the big moment plays again and again all evening.

Her diary of the day is on the app's home screen. And from there, the watch face goes onto your watch in a few taps.
