B1

The Last Herald

Released

Point-and-click adventure game

October 1987. Three kids from a Swedish sawmill town find a starship under a hill. It came down forty thousand years ago with a warning that was never delivered, and it hasn't given up.

Four acts in pixel art, every line spoken, in the spirit of the LucasArts classics. Free, with no ads and nothing to buy.

B3

Armada Zero: Firestorm

In development

Co-op pixel art space shooter

The Armada was built to end a war that nobody remembers. It slept for a thousand years. Now it is waking, system by system, and what is left to stop it is one strike wing flying ships that carry too many guns.

A roguelite bullet hell for one to four pilots: six weapons on one reactor, enemy fire that gets thicker every loop, and your friends on the same wifi.

  • Godot
  • LAN co-op
  • PC & Android
B4

Robin & Coco

Coming to Google Play

Watch face & live phone wallpaper

Robin was shipwrecked on a tiny island, in pixel art. Her only company is Coco, a coconut with a face, and a very good listener. Every day she has a plan to get off the island, and every evening it goes wrong.

She lives on your watch and on your phone, under the real sky and your weather. She works out when your heart rate is up, dances at your step goal, and sleeps after your bedtime.

B5

ThreadMix Auto DJ

Released

An AI DJ for your own music

A DJ that runs itself. Point it at your music and it listens to every track, then plays one continuous, beatmatched mix: key-aware blends, bass swaps, a voice on the mic and a light show.

Tell it "keep it deep" or "take it to peak time" and it plans the night. Everything runs on your own computer.

Audiobook reader for phone & watch

Liner notes

Read these while the tape rewinds

More than twenty years of building software. This is the side with my own name on the label.

I'm Peter Logarn, a software developer. Side B Labs is my independent studio: games, apps and devices that I design, write and publish myself.

So far that's a point-and-click adventure with every line voiced, a real-time audio engine in C++ that beatmatches by itself, a whole game ported to a 14 MHz Amiga, firmware for a microcontroller, and apps for Android and Wear OS.

Most of the tracks have a video on my YouTube channel. To get in touch, write in.
