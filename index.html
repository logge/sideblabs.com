B4

Robin & Coco

Coming to Google Play

Watch face & live phone wallpaper

A watch face and a live wallpaper about Robin, who is stuck on a tiny island with a coconut called Coco. Every day she tries a new way to get off the island, and every evening it fails.

She follows your day: the sky and the weather are the real ones where you are, she exercises when your heart rate is up, dances when you reach your step goal and goes to sleep after your bedtime.

B5

ThreadMix Auto DJ

Released

An AI DJ for your own music

When I was young I wrote several music programs: MP3 CD Organizer, to organize your MP3s, and a program for radio stations to play their music. I always wanted to automate beatmixing, and now the technology finally allows it.

ThreadMix analyses every track in your own music files and plays one continuous, beatmatched mix, with key matching, bass swaps, a voice on the mic and a light show. You can tell it things like "keep it deep" or "take it to peak time", and it all runs on your own computer.

  • Windows
  • Linux
  • Mac
  • Free

B6

ESP32 projects

On the bench

Small screens, small chips

My electronics projects. The first one is Gremlin, a little pixel art creature on an ESP32 with a 1.47-inch screen, which sits on the desk and reacts to what the computer is doing. It's happy when the tests pass, sad when the build fails, puts headphones on when music is playing and turns into a ghost when someone force-pushes.

B7

The Last Herald on the Amiga

Released

Commodore Amiga 1200 port

I ported the whole game to the Amiga 1200: all four acts, in 256 colours at 25 frames a second on a 14 MHz 68020. It needs 8 MB of fast RAM and a hard drive, or an emulator. I haven't tried it on a real Amiga yet, so tell me if you do.

  • 68020

B8

D&D character sheet

In development

A character sheet for the table

I play D&D myself, and with a busy schedule it's hard to keep all the rules in my head, so I made a character sheet that does it for me. One tab has what you need in a fight, and the others have your scores, gear, spells and traits. When the DM starts a session, everyone on the same wifi joins it and the phones stay in sync. It's part of a growing set of D&D apps that talk to each other.

  • Android
  • Kotlin
  • Works offline
